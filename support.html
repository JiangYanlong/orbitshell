Support Center

Support & Contact

OrbitShell installation, privacy requests, App Store review materials, and business inquiries — find the right entry point here.

Support email: support@orbitshell.cn Last updated: April 18, 2026

1. Common Entry Points

Product & Usage Issues

For installation help, connection configuration, feature inquiries, or bug reports, please email support@orbitshell.cn.

Privacy & Data Requests

To delete data, withdraw consent, or inquire about AI data processing, please read the Privacy Policy and contact us via email.

Legal & Compliance Materials

For App Store submission, enterprise procurement, or legal review, you may reference the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service on this site.

Business Cooperation

For custom deployment, team procurement, or partnership inquiries, please include your requirements and contact info in the email subject for faster response.

2. Recommended Info for Support Requests

  • Your platform and OS version, e.g. macOS, iOS, or iPadOS version.
  • OrbitShell version, when the issue occurred, and steps to reproduce.
  • Error screenshots or log snippets. Please redact any keys, tokens, customer data, or production addresses.
  • If related to App Store review, please also provide the review feedback原文 and relevant screenshots.

3. Pre-Submission Checklist for App Store

  • Ensure the Privacy Policy is a publicly accessible standalone URL, not just an in-app popup.
  • Verify that App Store Connect privacy labels match actual app behavior, covering all third-party SDKs and cloud services.
  • Confirm terms of service, support channels, demo accounts, review notes, and screenshots are complete.
