Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

OrbitShell is designed with local-first principles. Connection credentials and most configurations are saved on your device by default; data transfer only occurs when you actively use cloud capabilities.

Effective: April 18, 2026 Applies to OrbitShell client and orbitshell.cn

1. Scope

This policy applies to the OrbitShell application, official website, and support services directly related to product operation. By using OrbitShell, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the data processing practices described in this policy.

If a feature requires calling third-party services, this policy also covers data processing between the OrbitShell team and that third party for that feature; the service provider should provide data protection measures no less than those described in this policy.

2. What Data We Process

2.1 Data Saved Locally by Default

  • Server addresses, ports, usernames, connection groups, labels, and other connection configurations.
  • SSH private keys, passwords, database connection credentials, and other sensitive authentication information.
  • Terminal preferences, window layouts, favorites, and history records you actively save locally.

The above data is processed in a local-first manner. Sensitive credentials are encrypted and stored through system security capabilities, such as macOS Keychain or equivalent secure storage mechanisms.

2.2 Data That May Be Transmitted When You Actively Use Cloud Capabilities

  • Terminal output, error logs, command snippets, problem descriptions, or screenshot text that you explicitly submit in AI diagnosis, AI assistant, or similar features.
  • Network and device communication information necessary to complete the request, such as IP addresses, request timestamps, device types, and API call metadata.

If you do not actively use these cloud capabilities, the above content will not be automatically uploaded through OrbitShell's core connection features.

Important Notice: If you plan to declare "No Data Collected" in App Store Connect, please first confirm that the AI services, crash analytics, analytics SDKs, or customer support systems you integrate do not retain readable data outside the device; otherwise, you need to disclose accordingly.

3. How We Use This Data

  • Save your connection and preference settings locally to provide core features such as SSH, RDP, databases, and dashboards.
  • When you actively invoke AI features, send the content you choose to submit to the corresponding model service for returning diagnostic suggestions, explaining errors, or generating fix commands.
  • Maintain basic product security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and customer support.

Based on the current version's product design, OrbitShell does not process your data for selling personal information, cross-app tracking, or targeted advertising.

4. Third-Party Services & Sharing

OrbitShell only shares data with third parties to the extent necessary to complete features. For example, when you actively initiate AI diagnosis, the text you submit is sent to the third-party AI service provider integrated in the current version for real-time processing.

If you enable third-party analytics, crash reporting, customer support, or cloud sync features in a version, the corresponding data may also be processed by these service providers. The privacy labels you fill in App Store Connect must cover the actual data processing behaviors of OrbitShell itself and all third-party SDKs or service providers.

OrbitShell does not sell your personal information. Except as required by law, to protect user safety, investigate abuse, or fulfill contractual obligations, we will not disclose your data to unrelated third parties.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

5.1 Local Data

Connection configurations, credentials, and preference settings saved on your device will be retained until you actively delete them, reset application data, uninstall the application, or remove related entries through system security settings.

5.2 Cloud Request Data

OrbitShell itself does not retain content you submit to AI features long-term for account profiling or advertising purposes. If certain requests are processed through third-party infrastructure, that third party may retain necessary logs or request records in their system for a short period based on security, billing, anti-abuse, or troubleshooting needs; specific retention periods are subject to that third party's publicly available policies at the time.

5.3 Deletion Requests

You can remove local data through the in-app deletion feature, or send an email to support@orbitshell.cn to submit data deletion or privacy inquiry requests. If the request involves data processed by a third party, we will assist you in locating the corresponding service provider's deletion path or processing method.

6. Consent, Withdrawal & Your Choices

  • You may choose not to use AI diagnosis or any features that require network processing.
  • You may manually redact logs or commands before sending to avoid submitting passwords, keys, tokens, customer data, or other confidential information.
  • If a data processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw consent by disabling the corresponding feature, deleting local data, or contacting us.

7. Security Measures

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect data security, including local encryption storage, credential isolation, minimized transmission, and access control. Nevertheless, no network transmission or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security, so please avoid submitting high-risk production data without redaction.

8. Minors

OrbitShell is designed for developers, operations personnel, and enterprise technical users, and is not a product intended for children. If you believe a minor has provided us with data without guardian consent, please contact us at support@orbitshell.cn.

9. Policy Updates & Contact

If our data processing practices change, we will update the date and content of this page; when necessary, we will also synchronize disclosure adjustments in the app or App Store Connect's privacy information.

For privacy-related questions, deletion requests, complaints, or suggestions, please contact: support@orbitshell.cn.
